# Case Study Agreement

**Between:**
- **BitBrd** — Paul DeSana
- **West Little Rock Glass** — Gavin Higgins

**Date:** July _____, 2026

---

BitBrd rebuilt the West Little Rock Glass website and handles its hosting and online presence. That work is already underway and nothing about it changes here. This agreement covers one thing: **BitBrd may tell the story of that work as a case study.**

## What BitBrd may publish

- The business name "West Little Rock Glass" and its logo
- The city and state (Little Rock, AR)
- Before-and-after numbers — website traffic, search rankings, phone calls, and quote requests
- Screenshots of the website, analytics, and search results
- Photos of completed glass work from the website or provided by Gavin
- A plain description of the work BitBrd did

The case study may appear on bitbrd.com, in proposals, on social media, and in marketing materials.

## What BitBrd will never publish

Revenue, pricing, profit, customer names or lists, or anything Gavin asks to keep private.

## Testimonial

If Gavin chooses to give a testimonial, BitBrd may publish it with his name. Totally optional.

## The simple terms

This costs nothing and doesn't obligate anyone to anything else. Either side can end it with an email. Anything already published may stay published.
