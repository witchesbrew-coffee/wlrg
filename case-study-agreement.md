## 1. What BitBrd Is Providing

BitBrd will deliver the following services to West Little Rock Glass:

- **Website redesign and rebuild** — a new custom-built website replacing the existing Wix site at westlittlerockglass.com, including hosting migration, mobile-responsive design, contact forms, and search engine optimization. **Design, development, and migration are provided at no charge.**
- **Google Business Profile optimization** — tuning of the Client's existing Google Business Profile for local search visibility, at no charge
- **Search engine optimization (SEO)** — on-page SEO improvements including title tags, meta descriptions, structured data markup, and URL architecture, at no charge
- **Call tracking setup** — installation of tracked phone numbers to measure lead sources, forwarding to the Client's existing business line (501-223-3034), at no charge
- **Monthly performance reporting** — one-page monthly reports for a minimum of 90 days documenting traffic, leads, and search rankings, at no charge

### Hosting Fee

The Client's existing Wix.com hosting plan ($249.35/year) will be replaced with equivalent hosting managed by BitBrd at **the same annual rate of $249.35/year**. This fee covers domain hosting, SSL certificate, CDN delivery, uptime monitoring, and ongoing technical support.

- **No cost increase.** The annual hosting fee matches the Client's current Wix renewal rate exactly.
- **Current Wix renewal date:** January 11, 2027. The Client should **cancel the Wix plan before renewal** once the new site is live and confirmed stable. BitBrd will advise when it is safe to cancel.
- **BitBrd hosting billing cycle:** Annual, beginning on the date the new site goes live. First payment of $249.35 is due upon site launch.
- **Hosting fee includes:** Domain hosting, SSL/HTTPS, CDN, security headers, uptime monitoring, minor text/image updates (up to 30 minutes/month), and technical support via email.
- **Hosting fee does not include:** New page development, major redesigns, paid advertising, or services beyond routine maintenance. These will be quoted separately per Section 8.

## 2. What the Client Provides in Exchange

In exchange for the services described above, the Client grants BitBrd the following rights:

### a. Case Study Publication Rights

BitBrd may create, publish, and promote a **case study** based on this engagement. The case study may include:

- The business name "West Little Rock Glass" and its logo
- The Client's city and state (Little Rock, AR)
- Before-and-after performance metrics (website traffic, search impressions, phone leads, form submissions, search rankings, and similar digital marketing data)
- Screenshots of the website, analytics dashboards, and search results (with any sensitive data such as customer names or financial figures redacted)
- Photographs of completed glass installations currently displayed on the Client's existing website or provided by the Client
- A general description of services performed

The case study may be published on bitbrd.com, in proposals to prospective clients, on social media, and in marketing materials. BitBrd will **not** publish the Client's revenue, pricing, profit margins, customer lists, or any information the Client designates as confidential.

### b. Testimonial Rights

If the Client chooses to provide a written or verbal testimonial about the engagement, BitBrd may publish it with attribution. Providing a testimonial is **optional and not required** under this agreement.

### c. Reference Availability

The Client agrees to serve as a reference for BitBrd when contacted by prospective clients. Reference calls are **limited to two (2) per calendar quarter** and the Client may decline any individual request at their discretion.
