The goal

Grow organic search traffic by 25%

112 → 140
That is about 28 more high-intent local visitors every month, roughly 336 more per year, all of them people actively searching for the work you do. Organic search is already the engine of the website. Growing it is the highest-leverage way to bring more of the right customers to the shop, and it is what the new site was built to do.
Why 25%? It is a realistic 12-month target for a site rebuild plus local-search work, not a stretch. Local rebuilds commonly lift organic 20–50% over a year; we set the floor of that range so the goal is one we beat, not chase.
The finding that matters

Traffic has been leaking for a year

193 → 151
Summer and fall 2025 averaged ~193 visits a month. Spring 2026 averaged ~151 — a drop of about 22% year-over-year. This is what an aging website looks like in the data: rankings erode a little every month as competitors publish faster, more relevant pages. Nothing dramatic happens — the phone just rings a bit less each season. The rebuild is aimed directly at reversing this, first back to the old peak, then past it.

Visits are the start of a job

About 4 ready-to-buy local searchers find you on Google every day, for free.
Traffic is not the goal by itself; it is the top of the path to an install. Someone searches, lands on your site, then calls or sends a quote request, and that becomes a job. Growing organic search widens the top of that path — and because glass work runs from a $150 repair to a five-figure commercial job, a handful of extra searchers a week is real money.

Why it matters most

Organic search is the channel worth growing

It is the largest source of traffic by far, about 67% of every visit, and it has led every single month on record. Here is why it is the channel to invest in.

1

The highest-intent traffic there is

Someone searching "frameless shower doors Little Rock" or "glass repair near me" is looking to hire, not browsing. That is the most valuable visitor a local shop can get.

2

Essentially free, and it compounds

Unlike paid ads, you do not pay per click, and the work that improves rankings keeps paying off. Ads stop when you stop paying; rankings keep working.

3

The modern storefront sign

After 20+ years in the metro, ranking on Google for local searches is today's version of being the first name people think of. With ~95% of visitors brand new, search is how central Arkansas discovers you.

4

Trusted and measurable

People trust organic results more than ads, and we can see which searches bring people in and steadily improve. It is a lever we control, not luck.

By contrast, direct traffic (31%) is mostly returning customers, builders you already work with, and word of mouth — valuable and loyal, but it does not grow the customer base on its own. Referral traffic (2%) is minimal. Organic search is the one channel that both brings in new customers and can be deliberately grown.
How we get there

What drives the +25%

Every lever below is built into the new website.

01

A faster, modern site

Speed is a direct ranking factor. The new site is lean static pages instead of the heavier Wix setup it replaces.

02

Pages that match real searches

Five dedicated service pages (showers, mirrors, railings, storefronts, 24-hour repair) plus eleven city pages — the Little Rock metro plus Hot Springs, Greers Ferry, and Bentonville — give Google a strong page to rank for each search.

03

Search and local data built in

LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ structured data, unique titles on every page, a clean sitemap, and 301 redirects that preserve the old site's link equity.

04

Clean measurement in place

GA4 and Search Console are connected, and overseas bot traffic is blocked at the edge before it can pollute the numbers — so what we report is real customers.

What we need from you

One thing moves the needle most

★

Ask happy customers for a Google review

The biggest lever on searches like "glass company near me" is your Google Business Profile in the map results, and reviews are its fuel. You sit at 3.9★ across 38 reviews — and your recent reviews are 5★; the average is being dragged by old ones. If everyone who gets an install is asked for a quick review, the rating climbs, the map ranking climbs with it, and that compounds into calls the website alone cannot buy.
