## What we measured, and the sources

- **Source:** the current (Wix) website's built-in analytics. Real, historical numbers — not estimates.
- **Baseline window:** the most recent 12 full months, **July 2025 through June 2026**. This is the official "before" picture. (July 2026 is in progress and shown separately.)
- **Going forward:** **Google Analytics 4** and **Google Search Console** are installed on the new site, plus an edge-level filter that blocks non-US bot traffic before it can pollute the numbers. These are what we will use to measure the new site after launch.

## The goal: grow organic search by 25%

| | Current (monthly avg) | Target (+25%) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Organic search sessions | ~110 | **~138** |
| Organic search, per year | ~1,325 | **~1,655** |

That is roughly **28 more high-intent local visitors every month** — people actively searching for shower doors, mirrors, glass railings, storefront glass, and glass repair in central Arkansas.

**What drives the increase** (all built into the new website):

1. **A much faster, modern site.** Speed is a direct ranking factor; the new site is lean static pages instead of the heavier Wix stack.
2. **Pages built to match real local searches.** Five dedicated service pages (showers, mirrors, railings, storefronts, 24-hour repair) plus **eleven city pages** — the Little Rock metro plus Hot Springs, Greers Ferry, and Bentonville — so Google has a strong, relevant page to rank for each search.
3. **Proper search and local data built in.** LocalBusiness/Service/FAQ structured data, unique titles and descriptions on every page, clean sitemap, 301 redirects preserving the old Wix URLs' equity.
4. **Clean measurement in place.** GA4 + Search Console connected, with non-US bot traffic blocked at the edge so the numbers stay honest.

---

## Audience context (why this strategy fits)

- **~95% of visitors are brand new.** This is a discovery business; search is the discovery channel.
- **Device split is roughly even** — about half desktop, half mobile. The new site is fully responsive for both.
- **Quote requests and phone taps are now tracked** on the new site, so the next report can show leads, not just visits.
