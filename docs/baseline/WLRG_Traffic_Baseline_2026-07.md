> **The goal:** grow organic search traffic by **25%**, from roughly **112 to ~140 sessions per month**. Over a full year that is about **+336 organic visits**, on top of the current ~1,340.

> **The finding that matters most:** traffic has been **drifting down about 22% year-over-year** — summer/fall 2025 averaged ~193 sessions/month, spring 2026 averaged ~151. The old site is losing ground, not holding it. The new site's first job is to stop the slide; its second is to grow past the old peak.

Organic search is already the engine of this website. It brings in two of every three visitors. Growing it is the highest-leverage way to bring more of the right local customers to West Little Rock Glass, and it is what the new site was built to do.

## What we measured, and the sources

- **Source:** the current (Wix) website's built-in analytics. Real, historical numbers — not estimates.
- **Baseline window:** the most recent 12 full months, **July 2025 through June 2026**. This is the official "before" picture. (July 2026 is in progress and shown separately.)
- **A note on four months:** the Wix source-breakdown panel did not load for Aug 2025, Dec 2025, Jan 2026, and Mar 2026 when the data was captured, so those months show session totals only. The 67% organic share is measured across the **eight months with full source data** (1,376 sessions), and the monthly organic average is estimated by applying that share across the window.
- **Going forward:** **Google Analytics 4** and **Google Search Console** are installed on the new site, plus an edge-level filter that blocks non-US bot traffic before it can pollute the numbers. These are what we will use to measure the new site after launch.

## The trend: a slow leak the new site has to fix

| Period | Avg sessions / month |
|---|---:|
| Jul–Nov 2025 | ~193 |
| Feb–Jun 2026 | ~151 |
| **Change** | **−22%** |

This is what an aging site looks like in the data: rankings erode a little every month as competitors publish faster, more relevant pages. Nothing dramatic happens — the phone just rings a bit less each season. The rebuild (speed, dedicated service and city pages, structured data) is aimed directly at this.

---

## The goal: grow organic search by 25%

| | Current (monthly avg) | Target (+25%) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Organic search sessions | ~112 | **~140** |
| Organic search, per year | ~1,340 | **~1,680** |

That is roughly **28 more high-intent local visitors every month** — people actively searching for shower doors, mirrors, glass railings, storefront glass, and glass repair in central Arkansas.

**What drives the increase** (all built into the new website):

1. **A much faster, modern site.** Speed is a direct ranking factor; the new site is lean static pages instead of the heavier Wix stack.
2. **Pages built to match real local searches.** Five dedicated service pages (showers, mirrors, railings, storefronts, 24-hour repair) plus **eleven city pages** — the Little Rock metro plus Hot Springs, Greers Ferry, and Bentonville — so Google has a strong, relevant page to rank for each search.
3. **Proper search and local data built in.** LocalBusiness/Service/FAQ structured data, unique titles and descriptions on every page, clean sitemap, 301 redirects preserving the old Wix URLs' equity.
4. **Clean measurement in place.** GA4 + Search Console connected, with non-US bot traffic blocked at the edge so the numbers stay honest.

## What we need from Gavin: reviews

The single biggest lever on "glass company near me" searches is the **Google Business Profile Map Pack**, and reviews are its fuel. WLRG sits at **3.9★ across 38 reviews** — and the recent reviews are 5★; the average is dragged by old ones. A steady trickle of fresh reviews from happy install customers will move the rating and the Map Pack position faster than anything on the website itself. Ask at every completed install.

## Audience context (why this strategy fits)

- **~95% of visitors are brand new.** This is a discovery business; search is the discovery channel.
- **Device split is roughly even** — about half desktop, half mobile. The new site is fully responsive for both.
- **Quote requests and phone taps are now tracked** on the new site, so the next report can show leads, not just visits.
